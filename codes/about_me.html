“LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot must be used as a tool, not a crutch.”

I 100% agree with this statement. These powerful AI models can significantly enhance our productivity and creativity when used responsibly.

Heavy reliance on AI models without critical thinking can lead to a loss of essential skills and understanding. It's crucial to use these tools to augment our capabilities, not replace them.

When the use of AI models changes from “I'm using this to help me” to “I don't need to think, the AI will do it for me,” we risk losing our ability to reason, analyze, and make informed decisions.

I believe that the best approach is to use AI as a collaborative partner, leveraging its strengths while maintaining our own critical thinking and problem-solving skills.

Invest in What You Know

Gold slips away from the person who invests it in businesses or purposes they do not understand.

This law emphasizes the importance of investing in areas where you have knowledge and expertise. Investing in unfamiliar businesses or ventures can lead to poor decision-making and financial loss.

Just because an investment opportunity seems popular or profitable does not mean it is a good investment.
