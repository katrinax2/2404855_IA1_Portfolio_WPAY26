Why this Quote?

Sheryl Lee Ralph is a Jamaican actress and human rights activist. This quote reflects her commitment to fighting for justice and equality. This quote is a reminder that we must remain vigilant and proactive in protecting our rights and freedoms, as they can be threatened or taken away if we do not actively defend them. It emphasizes the importance of standing up for what is right and advocating for justice, even when it may be difficult or unpopular. This quote is particularly relevant in today's world, where issues of social justice and human rights are at the forefront of public discourse. It serves as a call to action for individuals to be aware of their rights and to actively work towards creating a more just and equitable society.

"LLM's such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool not a crutch."

I 100% agree with this statement. These powerful AI models can significantly enhance our productivity and creativity when used responsibly. Heavy reliance on AI models without critical thinking can lead to a loss of essential skills and understanding. It's crucial to use these tools to augment our capabilities, not replace them. When the use of AI models changes from "I'm using this to help me" to "I don't need to think, the AI will do it for me," we risk losing our ability to reason, analyze, and make informed decisions. I believe that the best approach is to use AI as a collaborative partner, leveraging its strengths while maintaining our own critical thinking and problem-solving skills. Control thy Expenditure
Budget your money and spend less than you earn. This is a fundamental principle of financial management. Saving money and living within your means is essential for building wealth and achieving financial stability.


5 Laws of Gold

Gold slips away from the person who invests it in businesses or purposes they do not understand.
Invest in what you know
This law emphasizes the importance of investing in areas where you have knowledge and expertise. Investing in unfamiliar businesses or ventures can lead to poor decision-making and financial loss. Just because an investment opportunity seems popular or profitable does not mean it is a good investment.
