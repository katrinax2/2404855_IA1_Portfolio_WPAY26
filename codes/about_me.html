My Favourite Idea from AI Business of 2025

The idea I really like is using AI apps to make healthcare more proactive instead of just waiting for people to get sick. These apps can track things like heart rate, sleep, workouts, eating habits, and medical history to spot potential problems early. In my life, I could use one with a smartwatch or fitness tracker to keep an eye on my health and catch issues before they get serious. This is super useful because it can help people stay healthier, save money on medical bills, and even prevent serious illnesses. For example, if the app notices something unusual in your heart rate or blood pressure, it could alert you to see a doctor sooner rather than later. Of course, there are ethical concerns. Health data is really private, and the app could make mistakes or give false alarms, which can stress people out. My solution would be to make sure all data is protected, let users control what they share, and always combine AI alerts with advice from real doctors instead of relying on the app alone. A real example is AliveCor KardiaMobile, which can monitor your heart and detect irregular rhythms using AI. You can check it out here: Alive Cor Overall, I think these apps are an exciting way to stay on top of your health, as long as privacy and accuracy are handled carefully.
